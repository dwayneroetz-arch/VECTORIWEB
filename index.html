04 — Purchase Readiness

Check the evidence before you commit.

A South African buying workflow should treat paperwork, roadworthiness, service history, licence status and ownership transfer as first-class decision inputs.

Buyer checklist

✓
Vehicle identity & history

Confirm VIN/vehicle details, service records and available history evidence.

✓
Roadworthy / registration evidence

For used-vehicle transactions, verify the documentation applicable to the transfer and current roadworthiness position.

✓
Licence status

Check that licence fees and registration information are current before finalising the transaction.

✓
Test drive & inspection

Do not allow a strong price score to replace physical inspection and a test drive.

VECTORI evidence rule

Every important claim should carry a provenance state:

OBSERVED
Directly supplied by an authorised source.
CALCULATED
Derived mathematically from supplied inputs.
ASSUMED
User-selected or model default that can materially change the result.
MISSING
Insufficient evidence. VECTORI should not fill the gap by guessing.

Data & legal architecture

1. Authorised data first

Production inventory should be obtained through an authorised dealer API, licensed feed, dealer CSV/JSON export or another contractual source that grants the necessary rights.

2. No automatic marketplace scraping

This prototype intentionally contains no automatic marketplace scraper. Production integrations should be based on explicit permission and contractual rights rather than assuming that publicly visible information may automatically be copied, stored or commercially reused.

3. POPIA

Personal information should be minimised. VECTORI's dealer inventory architecture should primarily process business and vehicle information rather than unnecessary personal data. Appropriate security, purpose limitation, retention and access controls should be applied in production.

4. ECTA and CPA

The production service should provide appropriate electronic disclosures, transparent commercial information and fair consumer-facing practices.

5. Finance calculations

Finance values shown by the prototype are mathematical estimates. They are not credit approvals, lender quotations or guarantees. Actual rates, fees, deposits, balloon payments, lender charges and credit assessments may materially change the result. Balloon finance is modelled as a residual amount due at term end; it is not free money.

6. Market evidence

VECTORI should distinguish between observed source data, calculated metrics, assumptions and insufficient evidence. A market benchmark must never be presented as fact when the underlying evidence is unavailable or inadequate.

7. Image rights

The vehicle illustrations used in this prototype are generated inline SVG demo illustrations. Production vehicle photographs should only be displayed when the relevant source or dealer agreement provides the required rights.

Production implementation should be reviewed against the applicable South African legal and contractual requirements before commercial launch.
