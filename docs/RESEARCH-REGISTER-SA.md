# VECTORI 2.0 — Finance & Outcome Research Register

**Research date:** 16 September 2026. Sources are official government/regulator/provider pages where available.

## Verified finance structures documented publicly

- Instalment sale / vehicle finance: Absa documents terms including 24–72 months and fixed or variable interest in its comparison material; FNB documents fixed/variable rate choice and balloon/residual structures; Standard Bank documents flexible terms and fixed/prime-linked structures; WesBank explains principal, interest, fees and balloon mechanics.
- Balloon/residual: Standard Bank and WesBank describe the lump sum due at the end of the term; WesBank documents settlement, trade-in and refinance pathways.
- GFV: WesBank documents guaranteed future value, mileage/condition requirements and replace/return/retain outcomes.
- Lease/rental: FNB documents dealership finance/lease use; Absa Islamic describes Ijaarah-style access to a vehicle for a fixed period and price.
- Islamic vehicle finance: Absa documents fixed/variable payment rental options, possible final/bullet payment and transfer of ownership under the agreement.
- Balloon refinance: WesBank documents refinance as a new agreement subject to a new application/affordability assessment.
- Personal loan/private-sale/specialised finance: WesBank publicly lists personal loans, private-sale finance, graduate finance, motorcycle/leisure finance and Islamic finance among its offerings.
- Business/asset finance: Standard Bank publicly documents vehicle and capital-asset finance, including lease, fixed/prime-linked rates, initial and balloon payment structuring.

## Verified insurance structures documented publicly

- Comprehensive motor cover is publicly documented by WesBank, Absa and Standard Bank.
- Limited/third-party cover is publicly described by Standard Bank.
- Credit shortfall/GAP-style cover is publicly documented by WesBank, Absa and Standard Bank.
- Return-to-invoice and deposit protection are publicly documented by WesBank and Absa.
- Debt/credit protection products are publicly documented by WesBank, Absa and FNB.
- Mechanical warranties, service plans, tyre/rim and scratch/dent products are publicly documented by major providers.
- Roadside/courtesy assistance is documented as an additional cover option by providers.

## Methodology rule

VECTORI must never use a stale web page or an inferred rate as a live product quote. Provider terms are stored as dated evidence and must be refreshed before production use.
